Tool usage measurement

Schroder Hill counts visible app page loads and report/PDF actions to understand which tools are useful. We record the action, time, country and approximate New Zealand region inferred by our hosting provider, Netlify, from the internet connection.

Regional locations may reflect a mobile provider or VPN rather than where you are. We do not request GPS, record your design location, or include design files, inputs, project names, contact details, city, precise coordinates or IP addresses in these event records. No visitor identifier or tracking cookie is created.

Netlify still processes requests, including IP addresses, to serve the site. Counts may include repeat use and undetected automation. Do Not Track is respected for these events.

We also count GeoJSON and local design-package download actions. These indicate a download was started, not that a file was saved or opened in GIS. Export contents and project locations are never included in these analytics.
